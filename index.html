The poet

Life & writing

Shakuntala Gupta writes in Odia and English. Her poetry moves through remembered places, longing, nature and the inner life.

Her writing appears in Kabita Live, Toshali Anthology of Poetry 2026 and magazine clippings she has shared from Akshyara and Matilagna. She has taken part in SAPNE literary activities and the Toshali Literature Festival.
